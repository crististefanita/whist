# Research and qualification of players

## Overview

Whist policy manager holds these research and qualification criteria. The rules already implemented are described in [decision model](../ai/ATF_DecisionModel.md), promotion follows [protocol offline](../learning/README.md).

## Playing for zero

| Priority | Risk | Closure criterion |
|---|---|---|
| P2 | Abstract weights can favour an arithmetic path hard to play. An example of a component does not determine the frequency of conversion to 500 or search cost. | Compare new paired matches with both schedules, 3–6 seats and streak bonuses enabled and disabled. Measure conversions to 500, target changes, ranking positions and latency before declaring strategic progress. |
| P2 | Rates in a controlled adverse prefix overestimate how frequently the opportunity occurs in the free game. Sampling may leave without support a possible arithmetic pathway. | Report the natural lot separately from the controlled lot, denominator, first election phase and conversions for each level. Keep larger lots with difficulty rotations and measure the cost for the hands where multiple targets are assessed. |

## Qualification contracts

The above open works are verified by responsible contracts; a change of documentation does not qualify the policy.

| Criterion required | contract and border |
|---|---|
| Information and model | [Behavior inference](../ai/ATF_RulesConfiguration.md#behavior-inference): reconstruction without duplication, without future information, with potentially undealt cards and missing, neutral or concentrated observation support. Individual factors do not certify the common distribution of opponents. |
| Independence of mechanisms | [Decision model](../ai/ATF_DecisionModel.md): separate legal restriction, voluntary plausible, continuation and utility; a sample of the helper remains limited to that API. |
| Guarantee and reserve | [AI components](../testing/ATF_AiComponents.md): allowed observation, independent quantifiers and explicit behaviour outside the area or budget exhausted. |
| Promotion and data | [Protocol offline](../learning/README.md#the-improvement-cycle): freezing, separation of the valuation adjustment and all the criteria declared; results and decision kept externally. |

Catalogue of honors and descriptive calibration have [units and preconditions](../ai/ATF_HandPatternsRo.md). Their documentary consistency and group mediation do not close the criteria of calibrated probabilities or strategic progress.
